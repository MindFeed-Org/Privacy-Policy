Our Core Commitment

MindFeed was built on a simple premise: reading should be peaceful, enriching, and free from algorithmic exploitation. We believe your thoughts and reading habits belong solely to you. This charter outlines our promises to writers, readers, and thinkers worldwide.

PART 1 · EDITORIAL CHARTER SECTION 01

Author Sovereignty & Intellectual Property

Authors are the lifeblood of MindFeed. We operate under a strict creator-first policy designed to respect and protect the creative work of our contributors.

✒️

Creators Retain 100% of Their Copyright

When you publish on MindFeed, you retain complete intellectual property rights to your writings, essays, and stories. We claim no ownership over your work.

📜

Non-Exclusive Publishing License

By publishing on MindFeed, you grant us only a non-exclusive license to format, syndicate, and display your article within our reader application. You are always free to republish, archive, or distribute your content anywhere else.

🛡️

Original Byline Protection

Author names, bios, and attribution are preserved across all reading views, archives, and search results. Bylines are never stripped or modified.

PART 1 · EDITORIAL CHARTER SECTION 02

Constructive Discourse & Community Standards

MindFeed is designed as a sanctuary for intellectual growth and quiet reflection. To preserve this sanctuary for all readers, we uphold clear community standards:

  • No Hate Speech: Content that threatens, demeans, or incites violence against individuals or groups is strictly prohibited and removed immediately.
  • No Defamatory or Unlawful Material: We do not host fraudulent claims, copyright violations, or malicious disinformation.
  • Spam & Manipulation Free: Commercial link stuffing, automated bot submissions, and artificial rating manipulation will result in immediate suspension.

PART 2 · PRIVACY CHARTER SECTION 03

The Zero-Tracker Guarantee

MindFeed does not participate in the digital advertising industry. We have deliberately omitted commercial tracking mechanisms.

ABSOLUTE PROMISE

"We do not sell, rent, or auction your reading history or personal data to data brokers, ad networks, or marketing exchanges. Period."

No Third-Party Ad Networks

We do not bundle ad networks, trackers, or behavioral pixels. Your device connects only to essential application services.

No Behavioral Profiling

We do not construct interest graphs, psychological dossiers, or predictive advertising models based on what you read.

PART 2 · PRIVACY CHARTER SECTION 04

Necessary Data We Store & Protect

In order to provide our reader features, authentication, and authoring tools, we process only the bare minimum of information required:

Information Purpose Protection Level
Account Credentials (Email & Password) To authenticate your account securely across sessions and allow password recovery. Strong Encryption
Author Profile & Stories Displaying your authored articles, draft manuscripts, and author biography. Controlled by You
Bookmarks & Stars Syncing articles you choose to save or highlight to your account library. Private to Account
Device Preferences Typography scale (Compact/Editorial/Large) and reading theme (Warm Paper/Dark). On-Device Only

* We never collect precise GPS background location, contact address books, or microphone/camera data.

PART 2 · PRIVACY CHARTER SECTION 05

On-Device Storage & Local Sovereignty

Your personal device is your reading sanctuary. When you download stories for offline reading, they remain strictly under your control.

📁

Instant Cache Erasure

MindFeed provides a dedicated "Offline Reading Archive" control directly in your app Settings. At any time, you can tap "Clear Cache" to permanently purge all offline downloaded articles and temporary files stored on your phone storage.

PART 2 · PRIVACY CHARTER SECTION 06

Account & Data Deletion Rights

You are always in control of your relationship with MindFeed. We respect your fundamental digital rights:

A

Right to Access & Edit

You may view, edit, or update your profile information, password, and published content directly within the application at any time.

B

Right to Full Deletion

You have the right to request the complete deletion of your account, authored stories, and any personal information linked to your email address.

SUPPORT & INQUIRIES SECTION 07

Editorial & Privacy Inquiries

If you have questions regarding this charter, author copyright, or wish to submit a data deletion request, our editorial team is available to assist you.
